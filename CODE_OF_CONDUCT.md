# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion technical. Harassment, personal
attacks, and discriminatory remarks are not tolerated. Maintainers may remove content or
contributors who violate this.

Because this project touches biometric analysis of real people, we hold an additional
standard: do not use the issue tracker or discussions to make claims about specific
individuals' honesty, and do not share recordings of people who have not consented.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers via GitHub.
